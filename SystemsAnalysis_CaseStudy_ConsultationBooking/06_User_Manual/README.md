# Deliverable 06 – User Manual and Quick Reference: Instructor Consultation Booking System

**Author:** Md Alamgir Hossain · October 2026 · *Illustrative case study: Walter Dvorak is the course instructor who acted as stakeholder. The interview answers and all figures here are illustrative examples prepared by the student team, not his actual statements.*

This guide shows students how to book, change and cancel a consultation, and shows the instructor how to manage availability, meetings and notes. It is the user documentation for the GUI prototype (Deliverable 08), following the user documentation guidance in Chapter 11.

## 1. Getting started

**Who this guide is for:** students and project groups booking consultations, and the instructor managing them. No technical knowledge is needed.

**What you need:** a college account and a web browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

**Sign in**

1.  Open the booking link from your course page in D2L Brightspace.

2.  Select **Sign in with college account**.

3.  Enter your college email and password. You stay signed in on that device until you sign out.

## 2. Student guide

**Book a meeting (S1 → S2)**

1.  On the **Booking calendar**, use the **\<** and **\>** arrows to choose a week.

2.  Open slots are shown in colour; booked or past slots are greyed out and cannot be selected.

3.  Select an open slot, then select **Book this slot**.

4.  On the **Booking form**, choose the meeting type: **Individual (15 min)** or **Group (30 min)**.

5.  For a group meeting, enter your group name.

6.  Choose **In person** or **Online (Teams)**.

7.  Type a short topic (up to 200 characters), e.g. "the case study feedback".

8.  Select **Confirm booking**. A confirmation message appears, and a confirmation email arrives within 1 minute.

**Reschedule or cancel (S3)**

1.  Select **My bookings** at the top of any screen.

2.  Find the meeting and select **Reschedule** or **Cancel booking**.

3.  To reschedule, pick a new open slot and select **Confirm**.

4.  You can change or cancel up to **2 hours** before the meeting. After that, email the instructor.

**Reminders**

- You get a reminder email **24 hours** before every meeting.

- Online meetings include a **Join Teams meeting** link in the confirmation and the reminder.

## 3. Instructor guide

**Set your availability (S5)**

1.  Select **Availability** in the top menu.

2.  Under **Office hours**, choose the days and times (default: Tuesday and Thursday, 1–3 pm).

3.  Set the **Slot length** for individual (15 min) and group (30 min) meetings.

4.  Under **Blocked dates**, add any days you are unavailable (e.g., reading week).

5.  Select **Save**. Changes show on the students' booking calendar immediately.

**Use the dashboard (S4)**

- **Today** lists your meetings in time order, with the student or group name, topic and location or Teams link.

- **Rest of the week** shows upcoming days and open slots.

- Meetings also appear in your Outlook calendar within 5 minutes.

**Add notes and mark status (S6)**

1.  Select **Open notes** on a meeting in the dashboard, or open **Notes and history** in the top menu.

2.  Type your notes in the **Private notes** box. Only you can see them.

3.  Select a status: **Held**, **Cancelled** or **No-show**.

4.  Select **Save notes**.

5.  To find past meetings, use **Search** or **Filter by group**.

**View reports (S7)**

1.  Select **Reports**.

2.  Choose **Monthly summary** (meetings held, cancellations, no-shows) or **No-show report** (only meetings marked as no-show).

3.  Select **Export** to download as Excel or PDF.

## 5. FAQ and troubleshooting

**Frequently asked questions**

| Question                            | Answer                                                                                      |
|-------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Why can't I select some slots?      | Grey slots are already booked, in the past, or on a blocked date.                           |
| How many meetings can I book?       | There is no limit in this version, but please book only what you need.                      |
| Can my whole group come?            | Yes. Book a **Group (30 min)** meeting and enter your group name.                           |
| Will other students see my booking? | No. Students see only their own bookings.                                                   |
| Who can read the meeting notes?     | Only the instructor.                                                                        |
| I didn't get a confirmation email.  | Check your junk folder, then check **My bookings**. If the meeting is listed, it is booked. |

**Error messages**

| Message                                | What it means                               | What to do                                                     |
|----------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| "This slot is no longer available."    | Someone else booked it a moment before you. | Pick another open slot.                                        |
| "Please enter a topic."                | The topic box is empty.                     | Type a short topic, then select **Confirm booking**.           |
| "Changes are closed for this meeting." | The meeting starts in less than 2 hours.    | Email the instructor directly.                                 |
| "Sign-in failed."                      | Your college login was not accepted.        | Check your email and password, or contact the IT Service Desk. |
